📖 DirectPay+ Guidelines

🏁 Getting Started

DirectPay+ is a business management tool that allows you to register your business, manage products, process payments, and track transactions — all in one place.

🔑 Quick Start:
  • Register your business using the ➕ New Business Registration button on the login screen.
  • After registration, log in with your Identifier (the 6‑digit or 9‑digit code you set).
  • Once logged in, you can manage products, generate QR codes, and process transactions.

📋 Business Registration

Complete the 4‑step registration process:

1. Info→ 2. Categories→ 3. Settings→ 4. Profile
  • Step 1: Enter business name, address, identifier, and contact details.
  • Step 2: Select up to 25 transaction categories (predefined or custom).
  • Step 3: Choose your mobile money provider (Orange or Afrimoney) and dashboard visibility.
  • Step 4: Upload a profile image or set an emoji for your business.

🛒 Product Management

  • Products are displayed in the scrollable strip on the main page.
  • Click any product to Buy & Customize — you can upload an icon, edit name/price/description, and set quantity.
  • After customization, you'll generate a payment USSD for the tiered customization fee.
  • Complete the payment, enter the transaction ID, and Save — the product icon will be updated after admin approval.

💳 Transactions & Payments

  • Select a product, then click 💰 Pay to generate a receipt and submit the transaction.
  • Transactions are stored locally and can be reviewed in the Admin Dashboard.
  • Admins can Approve or Reject pending transactions.
  • Approved transactions automatically replace the product icon.

👨‍💼 Admin Dashboard

  • Click the + in the header to open the Admin Dashboard.
  • Register the first admin account with a name and 4‑digit PIN.
  • Dashboard features: transaction management, product catalog, user list, activity log, and settings.
  • Admins receive SMS alerts for new transactions (if phone number is set in Settings).

📱 QR Code & Mobile Access

  • Each business has a unique QR code — click 📱 QR Code to generate and download.
  • Scanning the QR code with a mobile device automatically opens the business login page.
  • If the business is not registered, the QR scan will prompt registration.
⚠️ Important Notes:
  • Always double‑check phone numbers, amounts, and transaction IDs before submitting.
  • Mistakes may lead to failed transfers — use the Policy agreement as a reminder.
  • All data is stored locally in your browser. Clear browser data to reset.
  • For support, use the Contacts or WhatsApp buttons in the footer.
